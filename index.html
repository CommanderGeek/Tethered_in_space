TETHERED

Two ships. One tether. Everything in between gets sliced.

How to play
  • Fly: WASD or arrow keys (phone: drag on the left half of the screen)
  • Anchor: Space/Shift (phone: ⚓ button on the right). You get heavy and brake hard, so your partner swings around you.
  • Slice: Sweep the tether fast through asteroids. Too slow and they just bounce off.
  • Slingshot: Fly fast close past a planet (golden ring) for a short speed boost and double points.
  • Planets pull in you and the asteroids. Hard impacts cost hull.
  • Drones dodge the tether and shoot — the tether blocks their shots. Jellyfish split when sliced and sting.
  • Leviathan (every 5 waves) can't be sliced: squeeze it with the taut tether and orbit it together to wrap it up.
  • Careful: too much strain snaps the tether. Fly close together again to reconnect.
  • Quick slices in a row build up a combo.
